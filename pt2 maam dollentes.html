Governance of Basic Education

The DepEd Mandate

Tracing the historical foundations and statutory mandate of the Department of Education that shapes our operations.

Historical Evolution

1863

Education Decree of 1863

Established the Superior Commission of Primary Instruction, laying the initial organizational foundation for public education in the Philippines.

20th Century

Reorganizations & Charters

The educational agency underwent numerous restructuring efforts to align its purpose with changing administrations and national directions.

2001

Republic Act 9155

Enacted as the Governance of Basic Education Act of 2001, officially establishing the present-day mandate and highlighting the school as the heart of the formal education system.

Core Mandated Responsibilities

As an agency under the Department of Education (DepEd), we participate in its core institutional duties:

Policy Formulation

Formulates, implements, and coordinates policies, plans, programs, and projects for formal and non-formal basic education.

Institutional Supervision

Supervises all elementary and secondary education institutions, including alternative learning systems (ALS), across both public and private sectors.

Integrated Systems

Provides and maintains a complete, adequate, and integrated system of basic education relevant to the goals of national development.

Education is a shared responsibility. When the family, school, and community build alliances, we equip learners with essential knowledge, skills, and a sound value system.

Vision & Mission

Our commitments to academic advancement, cultural integrity, and stakeholder synergy.

Our Vision

The City of Bacoor National High School-Springville envisions Bacooreño learners who possess the value of patriotism, a sound value system, skills, and competencies that enable them towards self-actualization to contribute significantly in building a better community.

As a learner-centered public institution, CBNHS-Springville commits itself to academic advancement and character building to better serve its stakeholders.

Patriotism Self-Actualization Character Building Learner-Centered Academic Advancement

Our Mission

CBNHS-Springville is committed to safeguard and nurture the right of every Bacooreño Learner to excellent, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education.

Where every learner acquires knowledge in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating environment facilitated and nurtured by teachers — ensuring an enabling and supportive environment for well-organized learning to transpire.
